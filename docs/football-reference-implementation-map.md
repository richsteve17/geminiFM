## Reproduced numeric evidence

Using the actual hydrated Liverpool roster cloned as an equal-strength opponent and seeds 1–1000 (fixed lineup/condition, no AI prose): the old engine averaged **0.531 total goals**, **57.6% scoreless matches**, and **11.864 highlights**. The repaired xG benchmark model averaged **2.166 total goals**, **11.5% scoreless matches**, and **30.645 highlights**. Its mean total xG was **2.169826814**. Putting a striker in the goalkeeper's lineup slot yielded **0.709 home / 3.173 away goals**, instead of having no goalkeeper-specific consequence.

Known candidate xG scenarios (finishing and goalkeeper 80): central 6.3m without pressure **0.6083**; central edge-of-box 17.85m under pressure 0.8 **0.1021**; central 25.2m under pressure 0.9 **0.0246**. These meet the supplied approximate reference bands. These are simulation results, not claims about actual league statistics or commercial FM internals.
