# International gameplay repair — v3.3.0

## Required loop

World Cup is the tutorial and the credential for a new RTG career. A skipped tutorial permits unemployed/Championship starts only. Elimination opens lower opportunities including MLS. Winning opens elite clubs including Real Madrid. New-start selector, job generation, application and acceptance paths share the gate; manager-background roleplay does not grant a free elite reputation. Best completed results persist. Existing club careers retain earned mobility rather than being retroactively locked.

Intermediate round thresholds are explicit tuning defaults, not values supplied by the owner: group exit 65 reputation/MLS and Championship; round-of-32 70/prestige 75; round-of-16 75/80; quarter-final 80/85; semi-final 85/90; finalist 90/94; winner 100/all clubs. They live in `src/engine/tutorialProgression.ts`.

## International breaks as a reason to watch

National matches retain actual goals, penalties, takers, keeper duels, starters, substitutions, extra time and knockout outcomes. The Internationals view shows recorded results, participant lists, penalty sheets and replayable highlights. It does not resimulate a viewed match or mutate a club result.

Persistent return incidents attach to actual shared-club player identities. Tournament stage, humiliation, direct scoring/keeper involvement, decisive events and participant exposure determine severity. The real Madrid regression verifies that a Jude final hat trick generates a much deeper Jude/Mbappé feud than a Mbappé group-stage penalty against Courtois, while a non-scoring Tchouaméni is not blamed as the scorer. Shared national success and failure can create positive or negative teammate effects. Missing old match facts are not retroactively invented.

Manager mediation quotes and classifies the actual response through the existing server AI gateway; numerical outcomes remain deterministic relationship transitions. Severe cases retain resistance and memory. Benching separates selection without erasing the cause. Repeated genuine overlapping appearances can escalate to a transfer request, which connects to the existing incoming-bid/transfer-centre path. Tactical chemistry and positive training development consume bounded effects; a penalty no longer accidentally cushions a bad match performance.

When the manager exits the tutorial early, the remaining national tournament is completed using the existing deterministic football simulation, preserving observed fixtures. Thus a later national championship or failure can inform the club-return archive, without changing the manager's actual elimination reward.

## Reviewed defects resolved

One independent read-only review found: the domestic season-end branch intercepted World Cup week eight; Courtois was missing from a sufficient-sized friendly pool; background shootouts discarded named penalties; substitutions/co-selection relied on end-of-match starter flags; and training tags did not impair actual benched growth. All five mechanisms have been corrected. Regression tests cover the final guard, source identities, attributed evidence, genuine overlapping participation and positive training growth. Legacy club/unemployed saves and tutorial outcomes remain compatible, with compact source-pool-free serialization.
